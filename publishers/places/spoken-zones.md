**This addon is retired. Spoken Zones is now part of [Spoken](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken).**

Spoken carries Spoken Places (once Spoken Zones: zone and area lore on the world map) together with the other Spoken modules, in one download. This project gets no further updates.

## What to do

1. **Install [Spoken](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken).**
2. **Uninstall this addon**, and delete the `SpokenZones` folder from `Interface\AddOns` if it is still there. Its last update left a folder that never loads, listed greyed out as "Spoken Zones (now in Spoken)".
3. **Keep your voice packs.** [Spoken Audio: Places](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-audio-places) and the language packs work with Spoken unchanged.

If a full old copy is still installed, Spoken switches it off at login and offers **Reload Now**, so no line plays twice.
