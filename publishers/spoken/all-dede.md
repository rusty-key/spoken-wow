⚠️ NOT for manual download, install only via CurseForge app ⚠️

**Install this and everything is voiced in German.** It holds no audio and no code — it is a few kilobytes that names Spoken and every German sound pack as a dependency, so your addon manager fetches the lot.

## What it installs

[Spoken](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken), which carries Spoken Quests, Spoken Places and Spoken Readables inside it, and the German voice packs:

| Module | Voices | Its audio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spoken Quests | quest dialogue | [Spoken German Audio: Quests - Alliance](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-german-audio-quests-alliance)<br>[Spoken German Audio: Quests - Horde](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-german-audio-quests-horde)<br>[Spoken German Audio: Quests - Shared](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-german-audio-quests-shared) |
| Spoken Quests | NPC gossip | [Spoken German Audio: Gossip](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-german-audio-gossip) |
| Spoken Places | zone and subzone lore | [Spoken German Audio: Places](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-german-audio-places) |
| Spoken Readables | books, letters, notes and plaques | [Spoken German Audio: Readables](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-german-audio-readables) |

The quest dialogue comes as three packs, one per faction plus the quests both sides share, and this installs all of them: it does not know which side you play. Remove the other faction's pack afterwards if you want the disk space back.

## Language

Spoken speaks in your client's language by default. To hear German on another client, pick it under **Voice Language** on Spoken's settings page; **If a Voice Is Missing** decides what plays for a line the German packs do not hold.

English has its own set: [Spoken: English](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-english).
