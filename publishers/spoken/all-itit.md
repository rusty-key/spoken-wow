⚠️ NOT for manual download, install only via CurseForge app ⚠️

**Install this and everything is voiced in Italian.** It holds no audio and no code — it is a few kilobytes that names Spoken and every Italian sound pack as a dependency, so your addon manager fetches the lot.

## What it installs

[Spoken](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken), which carries Spoken Quests, Spoken Places and Spoken Readables inside it, and the Italian voice packs:

| Module | Voices | Its audio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spoken Quests | quest dialogue | [Spoken Italian Audio: Quests](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-italian-audio-quests-shared) |
| Spoken Quests | NPC gossip | [Spoken Italian Audio: Gossip](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-italian-audio-gossip) |

Spoken Places and Spoken Readables have no Italian voice pack yet, so they play in English, or not at all, depending on **If a Voice Is Missing**.

## Language

Spoken speaks in your client's language by default. To hear Italian on another client, pick it under **Voice Language** on Spoken's settings page; **If a Voice Is Missing** decides what plays for a line the Italian packs do not hold.

English has its own set: [Spoken: English](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-english).
