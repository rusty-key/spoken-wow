**This addon is retired. Spoken Books is now part of [Spoken Player](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-player).**

Spoken Player carries Spoken Books (books, letters, notes and plaques read aloud) together with the other Spoken modules, in one download. This project gets no further updates.

## What to do

1. **Install [Spoken Player](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-player).**
2. **Uninstall this addon**, and delete the `SpokenBooks` folder from `Interface\AddOns` if it is still there. Its last update left a folder that never loads, listed greyed out as "Spoken Books (now in Spoken)".
3. **Keep your voice packs.** [Spoken Books Audio](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-books-audio) and the language packs work with Spoken Player unchanged.

If a full old copy is still installed, Spoken switches it off at login and offers **Reload Now**, so no line plays twice.
