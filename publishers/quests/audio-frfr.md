Voiced quest dialogue for **[Spoken](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken)**'s Quests module, in French. This addon is data only — it does nothing on its own.

**Experimental.** This is a first cut, numbered 0.x until it has been listened through: expect lines to be re-recorded, and report the ones that sound wrong.

One download holds all four packs, each in its own folder: Alliance quests, Horde quests, the quests both sides share, and NPC gossip. They install beside the English packs rather than replacing them, so a player who switches languages keeps both.

## Installing

Install **Spoken** first; these packs are inert without it. Extract into `Interface/AddOns` and leave the folder names as shipped (`SpokenQuestsAudioAlliance_frFR`, `SpokenQuestsAudioHorde_frFR`, `SpokenQuestsAudioShared_frFR`, `SpokenQuestsAudioGossip_frFR`) — Spoken finds packs by folder name.

Spoken Quests speaks in your client's language by default. To hear French on another client, pick it under **Voice Language** on Spoken's settings page; **If a Voice Is Missing** decides what plays for a line these packs do not hold.

## Compatibility

For **WoW Forever**, Classic Era and Anniversary, with Spoken 3.0.0 or later.
