# Changelog — Spoken Everything: Quests, Zones, Books AI Voiceover

The meta addon: no audio and no code, only required dependencies. Versioned on its own, apart
from the player whose changelog is CHANGELOG.md beside this one.

## 3.0.0 — 2026-10-05

- Installs Spoken Player 3.0.0, which now carries Spoken Quests, Spoken Zones and Spoken Books,
  with the English voice packs. It no longer pulls in the retired Spoken Quests, Spoken Zones
  and Spoken Books downloads. If you have them, uninstall them and delete the `SpokenQuests`,
  `SpokenZones` and `SpokenBooks` folders from `Interface\AddOns`.

## 2.0.1 — 2026-10-02

- No longer listed as **Incompatible** on the Forever client after its update on 2 October.

## 2.0.0 — 2026-10-01

- First release. Installs Spoken Player, Spoken Quests, Spoken Zones and Spoken Books with
  every English sound pack: Quests Audio (Alliance, Horde, Shared Quests and Gossip), Zones
  Audio and Books Audio.
